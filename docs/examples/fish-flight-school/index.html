  2. FLT 201

    Leaving the Water

    The hardest four inches in aviation. Taught above a very soft pool.

    4 weeks · FLT 101
  3. FLT 301

    Emergency Splash Landing

    Everything that goes up comes down. We make sure it comes down wet.

    4 weeks · FLT 201 · towel required
